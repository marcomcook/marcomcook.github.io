Dr. Marco Cook

University of Glasgow
Glasgow Cyber Defence Lab

marco.cook@glasgow.ac.uk

I am a Research Associate in cybersecurity the University of Glasgow, where I am a senior member of the Glasgow Cyber Defence Lab (GCDL) and and the Networked Systems Research Lab (Netlab) in the School of Computing Science. My research advances the detection, mitigation and explainability of anomalies and intrusions, giving particular attention to Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS) and Industrial Control Systems (ICS) found in Critical National Infrastructure (CNI).

I recieved my Ph.D. from the University of Glasgow in 2023, which was funded through an EPSRC iCASE studentship award, partnered by the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl). My Ph.D. research investigated the intersect of intrusion detection and digital forensics for ICS, particularly the development of intrusion and anomaly diagnosis systems.
